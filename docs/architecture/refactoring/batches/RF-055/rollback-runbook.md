# RF-055 rollback runbook

On a trigger, stop further verification, retain evidence and volumes, rebuild the RF-054 Bank Channel/Gateway configuration, and replace only affected containers. Fixed synthetic Keycloak users may remain; they do not alter Fineract accounting. Do not delete historical business rows.
